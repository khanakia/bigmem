Before you pay — checklist & caveats

  • Confirm it's 128 GB, not 64 GB. The RAM is soldered and non-upgradable — a 64 GB unit is ~HK$8–10k cheaper and easy for a listing (or shop) to blur. The 128 GB SKU reads "128 GB LPDDR5X (16 GB × 8)".
  • "RAMpocalypse" (2026): LPDDR5X prices spiked, so 128 GB SKUs are ~40–60% above 2025 launch MSRP right now. Prices may ease later if you can wait.
  • 国补 subsidy prices are not for you. The sub-¥14,000 headline prices need a mainland ID + mainland delivery address — HK buyers can't claim the 15% subsidy.
  • Grey-market flag: the Price.com.hk EVO-X2 at ~HK$18–20k is dramatically under retail — likely parallel import. Verify the seller, warranty, and 128 GB config before trusting it.
  • Warranty math: HK retail (~HK$26.7k) buys a local 2-year warranty; mainland (~HK$16.2k) means self-service RMA. For a HK$25k box you rely on daily, weigh that ~HK$10k against your tolerance for shipping a dead unit back to Shenzhen.
